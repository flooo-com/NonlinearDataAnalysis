Introduction

Total solar irradiance (Data Set S1) during the Holocene (past 9300 years).
It is based on a composite described in Steinhilber et al. [2008] using 
cosmogenic radionuclide 10Be data as measured in the ice cores GRIP [Vonmoos 
et al., 2006] and SouthPole [McCracken et al., 2004], and neutron monitor 
count rates [Usoskin et al., 2005]. The data are 40-year running means and 
are resampled to a 5-yr time resolution. The time-scale of the GRIP data is 
ss09.

1. 2009gl040142-ds01.txt 
(Figures 1d and 2) Total solar irradiance during the Holocene.  
1.1 Column "year_BP", years before 1950.  
1.2 Column "dTSI", watts per squaremeter, difference of total solar irradiance

1.3 Column "dTSI_sigma", watts per squaremeter, 1-sigma uncertainty (shaded 
band in Figures 1d and 2).
